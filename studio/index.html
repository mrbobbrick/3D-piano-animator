Tips

  • A changed setting is marked with a dot. Its ↺ button (or a double-click) puts it back to the style's value.
  • Click a slider's number to type an exact value.
  • Reset puts back the open tab, every edit, or everything (Start over). Undo brings any of it back.
  • Hover a preview thumbnail for Use look (go back to its settings) and ✕ (delete it).
